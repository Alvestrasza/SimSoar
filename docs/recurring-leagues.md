# Recurring weekly and club leagues

Leagues create recurring UTC-based scoring rounds. Administrators configure the weekday, starting hour, and a duration from one to 168 hours, allowing continuous weekly rounds as well as shorter weekend windows.

A league can score public approved flights globally or restrict eligibility to current members of one club. Scores use either OLC points or distance. Flight upload, editing, moderation, deletion, and restoration recalculate league assignments. Changes to club membership recalculate affected active club leagues.

Each calculated window has a persistent round. Rounds close automatically when their end time passes and remain available in the public archive with their leaderboard and scored flights. The league page presents the current round when one is active and allows selection of prior rounds.

Administrators can create, reconfigure, activate, deactivate, and delete leagues. Deactivation closes active rounds without deleting their archived results. Management changes are audit logged.
